# F163 Transfer Forensic Report — 51,200 B over IONOS MPG@20, delivered 0.97 kbps

**Clock convention:** all times = Pi5 (sender) process seconds. Mac clock = Pi5 + ~3.7 s early-run drifting to +2.2 s late-run; all pairings below were made by content (ack base/bitmap/group_seq), not timestamps.

**Window reconciliation (where "423.8" comes from):** sender transfer window = first data TX 34.34 → final ack detect 469.02 = **434.7 s** (Pi5 GUI: "Transfer complete 434.7s, 0.94 kbps"). The quoted **423.8 s / 0.97 kbps** is the receiver-side measure (Mac clock, first-delivery → file-CRC-ok at mac 470.781). The 10.9 s difference is measurement-window definition, not unaccounted time. The budget below sums to the 434.7 s sender window; percentages apply to either within ±2.5%.

**Frame/byte conversion:** 107 unique frames (seqs 0–106) carried the 51,200 B file → **478.5 B per delivered frame** (empirical bytes/base-advance). Every data frame occupies a fixed 59,360-sample slot = 1.237 s regardless of rung (sp8 fixed grid, verified sample-exact on all 38 bursts).

## 3. Top 5 time sinks (excess vs the healthy 10.4 s-per-5-frames cadence)

1. **16QAM R2/3 adoption black hole (P6) — ~58 s lost.** The up-switch rode a 0.21 s light descriptor into a fade; the receiver never adopted the new rate, three whole bursts failed 0/16 CW while chirp sync held lock on dead LLRs, and the COLLAPSE-escape fired 66 ms after queuing an 8.79 s RTO resend, dooming it at queue time (16.4 s continuous keydown). Ledger status: mode-switch-doomed-bursts KNOWN; the **never-adopted-descriptor black hole and the doomed back-to-back double burst are NEW**.
2. **seq-5 era stall / voided 8PSK excursion (P2) — ~50 s lost.** Rate down-switch MOVE-EPOCH voided 7 already-SACKed frames from two 3/5 and 4/5 partial groups, and the epoch adopted unanchored on the receiver → ack-silent WAITING-REBASE saga plus a 34-event full-anchor-wait storm. Ledger status: mode-switch doomed bursts — KNOWN (this run adds the SACKed-then-cleared count: 13 frames received on air then discarded).
3. **Missed light descriptor #1, 8PSK (P4) — ~41 s lost.** The 0.21 s warm descriptor was missed, so the receiver decoded the entire 12-cw 8PSK group at its stale 8-cw QPSK config (proven by cw_fail=8), and rate adoption waited 25.8 s for a full-anchor RTO resend that itself cratered. Ledger status: **NEW mechanism** (adopt latency 3–7 s when the descriptor decodes vs 25–26 s when missed).
4. **Missed light descriptor #2, 16QAM (P8) — ~30 s lost.** Identical mechanism (cw_fail=12 vs a 16-cw burst), adoption latency 24.5 s, plus a post-adoption 0/5 crater. Same NEW mechanism as #3 — two occurrences in one transfer; the two light-descriptor bursts that WERE adopted cleanly (160.5, 273.3) cost ~0.
5. **Normal half-duplex ack turnaround — 91.4 s total (21%).** ~3.4 s per cycle × 34 cycles: 2.0–3.4 s ack detection latency after burst end + 408 ms ack tone + T/R. Structural cost of the 5-frame group size; KNOWN ledger item (the turnaround lever). Note the 51.5 s of RTO dead-air (row 6) is fully contained inside sinks 1–4 and is a *timer-length* problem: the RTO fires 11.4 s after burst end while a healthy ack arrives in 2–3.4 s, so every crater costs ~8 s of pure deadness beyond physics; also the 346.45 RTO **ran from submit time, not air time** (fired while its predecessor was still airing) — NEW.

## 4. The one number

Because the sp8 fixed grid pins every data frame at 1.237 s and the delivered payload averaged 478.5 B/frame **at every rung observed** (base advanced 5-per-group identically at QPSK R1/2 and 16QAM R2/3), the empirical per-rung delivered ceiling is rung-flat in this run — the ladder's up-switches bought zero throughput upside here, only stall downside.

- Empirical clean-group ceiling (delivered bytes per clean group ÷ full cycle incl. descriptor + turnaround): 2,393 B / 10.4 s = **1,841 bps**.
- **Ideal time for 51,200 B at the rungs actually used = 51,200×8 / 1,841 = 222 s.**
- **Actual = 423.8 s (receiver measure) / 434.7 s (sender window) → efficiency ratio = 222/423.8 = 0.52 — the transfer ran 1.9× slower than its own demonstrated clean cadence.**
- For reference, the pure-airtime ceiling (no turnarounds, minimal descriptors: 132.3 s data + 31.0 s descriptors = 163 s ≈ 2,513 bps) gives efficiency 0.39; the missing 48% between ideal and actual is exactly the four stall windows of §1 (210.4 s for 15 frames, ~179 s excess).
