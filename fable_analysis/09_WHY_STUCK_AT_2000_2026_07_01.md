# 09 — Why we are stuck at ~2000 bps, re-analyzed (2026-07-01)

**Audit:** Claude Fable 5, 2026-07-01. Question asked: *"we ran a lot of tests before — check the
code as to why we are stuck. Is it a sync issue, a real fading issue, what? QPSK above R2/3 gives
too many retries on Good fading; the leader does ~3000, our best is ~2000."*
Method: defaults sweep + verified cycle-arithmetic model (every constant file:line-checked) +
4-agent code audit with adversarial cross-examination + fresh paired GUI-gate runs on HEAD
(a81725d) + live IONOS MPG@20 rig runs (Mac↔IONOS↔Pi5, both ends at HEAD) + a 3-agent
fact-check/completeness/arithmetic review of this document itself. Supersedes the 06-12
roadmap's Phase-2a/2b framing (docs 00–08 remain the historical record).

---

## 1. The direct answer

**It is not a sync issue on the clean path.** Empirically verified on this session's runs:
chirp/LTS acquisition is essentially lossless for QPSK (24/24 groups light-anchored, 2 warm
misses over a whole transfer, 0 timeout resends, ACK leg 25/25 heard; the decoder ran ahead of
live audio throughout — SKIP-unsearched pattern). Warm-sync, anchor-skip, and the ACK monitor
work as designed. **Scope caveat:** on the real rig, the 16QAM route IS currently blocked by a
burst-*acquisition* collapse (§4, BUG-QAM16-RIG-ANCHOR-COLLAPSE) — a sync-front failure on the
dense rung specifically, though not yet proven a sync-algorithm defect.

**It is a fading issue only in the irreducible sense.** Good fading erases deep-nulled symbols;
~25% FEC redundancy (R3/4) sits at that cliff edge, thinner FEC is below it. The retries you see
at QPSK R3/4+ are the channel's bill, correctly collected by ARQ — not a defect. eps_H, LLR
recalibration, and frequency diversity are measured-exhausted levers (06-14): there is no
estimator fix that removes fade loss.

**The actual jailer is cycle arithmetic.** The verified zero-retransmission ceilings
(delivered file bits — after LDPC byte-flooring, 19 B frame overhead, 5 B chunk overhead —
steady-state anchor-skip K=2, tone-ACK at the 324 ms bin, T = non-ACK dead time per cycle;
independently recomputed and confirmed exact):

| Config (cw8, sp8) | Frames/burst | Ceiling @T=0.7s | @T=1.0s | @T=1.5s | Binding cap |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| QPSK R2/3 (stock pick) | 5 | 1898 | 1834 | 1736 | 8.6 s airtime budget |
| QPSK R3/4 | 5 | 2121 | 2049 | 1940 | 8.6 s airtime budget |
| 16QAM R2/3 | 8 | 3429 | 3299 | 3103 | ARQ window = 8-bit SACK mask |

(At the ACK bin actually measured today — 675 ms, §3.3 — the 16QAM T=1.0 ceiling is ~3158.)

- **QPSK measured 2090/2000/1690 (3 seeds, Good@20, 50 KB) — the clean seeds operate at
  ~95-100% of ceiling** (seed 7's 1690 ≈ 80% paid extra fade retx). Nothing is left to tune at
  QPSK; 3000 bps is arithmetically excluded at any QPSK rung in this frame design (in-frame
  nets: R2/3 2717, R3/4 3057 bps at 100% airtime — and half-duplex forbids ~100%).
- **16QAM R2/3 measured 1760/1990/1890 — 53-60% of its 3.3k ceiling.** The missing part is the
  fade-retransmission tax at its zero-margin anchor: 47% of frames fail per pass (113 deint-FAIL
  vs 127 OK on seed 42) → ~31% of TX airtime is resends. Sync clean, ACKs 29/29.
- **Margin sensitivity (Good@22, same seeds): 2710/2150/2050 — mean +22%, seed-42 damage
  collapsed 113→30 fails (82% of ceiling).** The QPSK↔16QAM crossover sits between ~20 and
  ~22 dB effective; above it 16QAM beats ceiling-bound QPSK outright. Channel-limited at the
  anchor, not mechanism-broken.
- The "stuck at exactly ~2000" experience = QPSK at its ceiling, and 16QAM taxed back down to
  the same number. The June-17 paired A/B (tie at ~2100) measured the same thing. On the rig,
  half the time the 2 kbps path is never even entered (§4 coin-flip) — "stuck at 2000" is the
  *good* half.

**Why you're stuck at R2/3 specifically (stock):** `ULTRA_R23_BASIS` (default-ON) pins the
connect-time entry at QPSK R2/3 on any fading channel at SNR≥18, and the rate controller that
could climb to R3/4 is default-OFF (`ULTRA_RATE_ADAPT`). Backed by measurement (R3/4≈R2/3 on the
rig, +6% = noise; the sim-side tie is the June-17 paired A/B — this session's same-seed stock
pair is confounded by the §3.2 saga), so unpinning it buys ~nothing — but out-of-box the modem
literally cannot leave R2/3.

> **BENCHMARK RE-ANCHORED FIRST-PARTY (2026-07-02):** the Winlink dev team's own sim sheet
> ("VARA_Sims_2025", Rick Muething KN6KB — the IONOS box's author — updated 11/17/2025,

> **VARA HF 4.8.9, 3 kHz, MPG@20 = 23,142 bytes/min = 3,086 bps DELIVERED** (user-confirmed
> cell). Same channel definitions as our bench, same end-to-end measurement basis as our
> GOODPUT_BPS — the June audit's "accounting is not the gap" verdict is now first-party-
> confirmed, and yesterday's "scoreboard convention" discount is fully retracted. The
> 3,086@MPG@20 target is real, measured, and achieved through the SAME fade troughs that
> kill our fixed rungs — i.e., by continuous fine-grained rate/mod adaptation (fade-cycle
> riding), which is the campaign's redirected objective.


Our wideband numerology (59 carriers × 41.67 baud, FFT1024+CP128) already **is** the leader's
multipath-rung geometry — the raw playing field is level (raw QPSK symbol capacity
59×41.67×2 ≈ 4917 bps). The differences that matter:

1. **Scoreboard convention (state this first — it is part of the "gap").** The leader's "3000"
   is their displayed net link rate; their *delivered* goodput is estimated ~2700-2900, and
   their long-key-down operation implies a duty profile our deliberate 8.6 s PA cap refuses.
   Our numbers here are end-to-end delivered on a 0.61 dB harder SNR convention. Under their
   convention, our QPSK R3/4 already "nets" 3057 — so roughly a third of the headline gap is
   convention, and the rest is real and enumerated below.
2. **They ride the modulation ladder above QPSK only above its crossover.** Their ~3 kbps
   multipath rung is QPSK; QAM16 is their near-top rung for clean/high-SNR channels.
3. **In-frame overhead:** our QPSK R3/4 in-frame rate is 62% of raw — decomposed exactly as
   code rate 0.75 × pilots 51/59 × LTS 51/53 (the 19 B header + 5 B chunk + byte-flooring cost
   sits on top: net-of-everything 2868 bps = 58% of raw). The leader's equivalent framing is
   probe-lean (~78%, basis unverifiable — treat as indicative). Our 16QAM frames are also only
   672 ms, so the dense rung pays the per-frame overhead twice as often as QPSK.
4. **Their high rungs assume energy accumulation and deep interleaving** (mil-standard QAM16
   rungs ship with multi-second interleavers and dense known-symbol probes). We have the
   interleaver (5.4 s ≈ 1.3 Tc — adequate) but **our HARQ does not accumulate on the frames
   that matter** (§3.4).

## 3. What the code audit found (adversarially cross-examined)

Four auditors + four adversarial reviewers + a document-level fact-check. Everything below
survived refutation; corrected numbers shown.

### 3.1 Clean-path turnaround is already near its floor
Itemized minimum in today's code: **~620-810 ms at the 324 ms ACK bin** (~970-1160 ms at the
675 ms occupancy actually measured, §3.3): 150 ms ACK lead-in + tone ACK + ~80 ms detect
cadence + ~50-100 ms encode + 150 ms data lead-in. The 1500 ms SACK hold is NOT on the burst
path (verified against the stale comment at `connection_policy.hpp:1013-1015`). Component
arithmetic reconciles the 06-20 OTASim 0.61 s anchor, though the total has not been measured
as a single unit. Rig measures ~1.5 s; the delta is soundcard buffering + inter-group jitter.
⚠ Do NOT trim the ACK lead-in naively: it covers the data-sender's T/R + buffering before the
Costas head arrives (CHANGELOG 482a285 caveat) — an asymmetric reliability downside for +59 bps.

### 3.2 NEW DEFECT — BUG-BURST-HEADNULL-DROP (filed): group-head-null silent drop
`default_adaptive_s42` lost 83 s delivering one ~8 s group. Mechanism (code + log verified):
burst accumulation is **marker-gated** — only the group-start frame (negated-LTS marker) can
enter accumulation (`streaming_ofdm_decode.cpp:1271` entry; marker check ~:1090). When a fade
null covers the group head, frames 2..N arrive at corr 0.95 / SNR 27 dB, are sync-accepted, hit
the 1-CW control-first peek, and are **silently consumed by the log-less mid-burst re-search**
(`streaming_ofdm_decode.cpp:1042-1052`) — no 8-CW decode attempt, no counter, no partial-ACK
credit. ~20 s of clean frames were received and discarded; each occurrence costs one full
~24.5 s RTO cycle (≈8 s of it the wasted group re-send, ≈16 s dead wait). Note the existing RX
group-timeout fast-NACK does NOT cover this case (it requires the BURST_HEADER *and* ≥1 decoded
data frame). The silent path exists for a documented reason (the legacy fall-through poisoned
the burst's shared channel estimate, §14.24/14.25) — the fix must re-demodulate from the ring at
the data profile, not fall through; and the descriptor already gives the receiver the group
geometry, so accumulation could be entered from ANY group-member sync with the head
erasure-marked (1-frame nack instead of a saga). The missing *counter/log* is free — land it
regardless.

### 3.3 NEW DEFECT — BUG-ACK-STAIRCASE-FADE-BIN (filed): the fast tone-ACK almost never engages on fading
The SNR-adaptive ACK staircase (§15.5; `tone_burst_constants.hpp:99-105`) picks 12 ms symbols at
≥18 dB — but the receiver feeds it the **fade-effective** in-band SNR (`app.cpp:645-651`), which
typically reads below the AWGN-calibrated 18 dB edge at Good@20 (~16-17, Jensen penalty).
Measured occupancy this session: 3 of 4 runs at 0% fast-bin — sim 16QAM 29/29 ACKs at 675 ms,
stock 26/26, **rig MPG@20 30/30**; only the QPSK R3/4 sim run crossed the edge (20/25 at
324 ms). The hardware-proven fast rung is dormant at the operating point it was built for:
~350 ms extra per cycle ≈ 4-5% goodput, taxing the short-burst dense rung hardest. Fix: express
the bin edges in fade-effective terms (~16 dB edge) or feed the calibrated basis; validate ACK
FER at the new edge on Watterson first.

### 3.4 NEW FINDING — HARQ chase-combining is blind exactly where 16QAM needs it
Soft-combine requires the current attempt's CW0 to decode to build the combine key
(`streaming_ofdm_decode.cpp:2933-2941`); a deep-nulled frame stores nothing, so **resends of the
dominant Good-fade loss class decode standalone** — no energy accumulation across attempts
(instrumented: `harq_key_build_failed`, :2978-2986 — read it from a Good@20 16QAM run to size
the win before building). This is the sharpest difference between our 16QAM and a mil-style
QAM16 rung. ⚠ The code deliberately rejects receive-order provisional keys (collision → false
chase-combines, worse than losing the copy) — a viable key must come from ARQ/descriptor
context (e.g. the BURST_HEADER's announced group seqs + in-group position) and must answer that
objection explicitly.

### 3.5 Lever — the airtime budget is blind to anchor-skip (+6-8% expected on QPSK)
`burstAirtimeBudgetFrames` always charges the 1200 ms full anchor; anchor-SKIPPED (light) bursts
could legally carry a 6th QPSK frame but never do (`connection_policy.hpp:630-638`,
`connection.cpp:3285-3303`, skip decided later in `streaming_encoder.cpp:602-618`). Frame math
verified deterministic; the goodput delta depends on the light-burst fraction — measured today
(descriptor signature: 67680-sample full vs 10080-sample light): sim QPSK R3/4 42% skipped, sim
16QAM 34%, sim stock (saga run) 28%; rig QPSK 43%, rig stock 43%, **rig 16QAM 21%**. The
reactive gate (streak=4; any resend/cold/escalation resets) converges to ~43% on clean runs and
self-disables under distress — correct for safety, but it makes the dense rung pay near-full
anchor cost on top of its retx tax (a third compounding 16QAM penalty besides the 675 ms ACK
bin and the budget blindness). The §1 ceilings assume the ideal 600 ms steady-state average;
real average anchor is ~750-1100 ms depending on run health.

### 3.6 Retransmission economics are otherwise sound
Per-frame SACK accounting is intact on the unified path (the "interleave ⇒ whole-group ACK"
belief is stale — a nulled physical frame enters deinterleave as erasure; only failed logical
frames are resent, riding the next budget burst at ~1.0× amplification). The all-or-nothing
16QAM group loss is **PHY correlation** (interleave spreads a null across all logical frames),
not protocol amplification. Remaining timeout conservatism: the RTO double-counts burst airtime
(BUG-ACK-TIMEOUT-DOUBLECOUNT, ~24.5 s vs a principled 14-17 s — that floor is itself pending
rig worst-case-turnaround calibration; calibrate before moving). The receiver's wall-clock
group timeout (8000×n/4 ms, `streaming_decoder.hpp:814`) is not mod/rate/z-derived — an
adaptivity violation with ~4.5 s margin today; tighten jointly with the RTO or it will
spuriously NACK.

## 4. The rig at MPG@20 (live, both ends at HEAD — #70 robust-PING and #74 ratiometric SNR default-ON, so stock #1's pick below is purely the #58 policy hole, not handshake residue)

| Run | Mode negotiated/forced | Result |
|---|---|---|
| stock #1 | snapshot 12.4 dB + fading 0.67 "Moderate" → **DBPSK R1/4 (Robust-Mid, ~94 bps nominal)** | **0 bytes in 9 min** |
| stock #2 | snapshot 18.2 dB + fading 0.39 "Good" → QPSK R2/3 | CRC ok, **1.53 kbps**, 18 retx |
| forced QPSK R3/4 | — | CRC ok, **1.62 kbps**, 21 retx |
| forced 16QAM R2/3 | — | **FAIL — no delivery in 480 s**; 117 nack + 74 timeout retx; **0 PHY frame failures** (77/77 deinterleave SUCCESS) |

**The out-of-box experience is a coin-flip between ~1.5 kbps and a ~94 bps-nominal rung that
delivered zero bytes** (~16× nominal spread; unbounded in delivered terms). Sync SNR read
21.8 dB — the channel was fine; the 170 ms connect-time snapshot read 12.4 dB during a fade dip
and the blind classifier simultaneously read "Moderate", raising the OFDM entry floor to 14.
This is open task **#58** (fade-averaged connect SNR) compounded by the known classifier
coin-flip. On a real radio this failure mode *is* "why are we stuck" — half the time the 2 kbps
path is never entered. R3/4 vs R2/3 on the rig: +6% = noise (reconfirms rate-is-not-the-lever).

**NEW rig-only finding — BUG-QAM16-RIG-ANCHOR-COLLAPSE (filed).** The 16QAM run decoded 9+
groups cleanly (77/77 deinterleave SUCCESS — no fade-damage tax at all on the rig this hour),
then fell into a persistent saga: sender escalates to full-anchor resends, receiver sits in
`Full-anchor wait rejected DATA fallback (corr=0.34 < 0.50)`. Sync-correlation histograms are
night and day on the same channel minutes apart: QPSK R3/4 run corr modes **0.92-0.99**; 16QAM
run corr modes **0.16-0.29**. The PHY is fine; the bursts stop being *acquired*. Two candidate
mechanisms (root cause NOT yet isolated): (a) TX-side — 16QAM+interleave raises burst crest
factor → per-burst hardware normalization drops average power → the in-burst anchor chirp sinks
toward the noise floor (the 2026-06-16 IONOS PAPR mechanism; `ULTRA_SIM_PAPR_PENALTY` exists
because sim cannot show this); (b) RX-side — decode/search backlog during the saga correlating
against stale buffers (#56 class), locked in by the full-anchor-wait rejection threshold.
Discriminating experiment: log the Pi5 per-burst normalization factor + Mac chirp-segment RMS,
paired QPSK-vs-16QAM. **Consequence: on real hardware today, 16QAM's blocker is not fade damage
— it is this acquisition collapse plus the §3.2/§3.6 saga economics.**

## 5. The corrected path to 3000 (replaces 06_ROADMAP Phase 2a/2b)

QPSK is closed (at ceiling). The only route is 16QAM R2/3 (ceiling 3.3-3.4k at cw8; ~3.9k at
cw16) with its damage bill paid down, plus percent-level cycle fixes. Ranked by
(expected bps × confidence)/cost:

1. **Reliability first (per PROJECT_GOALS): fix the connect-time coin-flip (#58).** Fade-average
   the connect SNR over the whole training sequence + hysteresis; a wrong pick costs ~16×
   nominal (zero delivered on the rig), every day, on real radios. Stock behavior at MPG@20
   must not be a ~94 bps-nominal rung that delivers nothing. *(policy change + rig A/B)*
2. **ACK staircase fade-basis fix (§3.3): +4-5% everywhere, trivially safe.** One threshold
   expressed in the right units; validate ACK FER at the 16 dB edge on Watterson first (each
   lost ACK costs an RTO).
3. **Group-head-null drop (§3.2): counter+log now; recovery fix next.** Converts rare 30 s
   sagas into 1-frame nacks; biggest effect on stock/QPSK tail latency and transfer-time
   variance.
3b. **BUG-QAM16-RIG-ANCHOR-COLLAPSE diagnosis (§4) — gate for everything 16QAM on hardware.**
   One instrumented paired rig session (per-burst normalization factor + chirp-segment RMS/corr)
   decides PAPR-vs-backlog. Until closed, all 16QAM throughput work is sim-only by definition.
4. **HARQ CW0-independent combine key (§3.4): the 16QAM damage attack.** Read
   `harq_key_build_failed` from a Good@20 16QAM run FIRST to size the win; the key design must
   answer the code's documented collision objection (ARQ/descriptor-derived, not receive-order).
5. **cw16 frames for 16QAM (build): raises the 16QAM ceiling to ~3.9k.** 16 CWs/frame gives
   16QAM the same 1272 ms frame airtime as proven QPSK cw8 geometry (same coherence exposure,
   half the LTS/header overhead per bit). Currently capped by `kMaxFixedFrameCodewords=8`
   (`frame_v2.hpp:1031`). ⚠ Corrected arithmetic: **5-frame groups** under the current budget
   (a full-anchor 6-frame cw16 group is 9.2 s key-down — over the duty cap, ILLEGAL); 6 frames
   ride only anchor-skipped bursts and only after item 6 lands. Ceilings: current-code 5-frame
   3907/3775/3574 at T=0.7/1.0/1.5; with item 6, alternating 5/6: ~4002/3876/3683.
   ⚠ cw16's hardware viability pends 3b's verdict — if the collapse is PAPR/normalization,
   longer interleaved 16QAM frames plausibly worsen it.
6. **Budget-aware anchor-skip (§3.5): +6-8% expected on QPSK rungs** (helps the fallback rung
   and stock; also unlocks cw16's 6-frame light bursts).
7. **RTO + group-timeout joint tightening (24.5→~16 s):** halves the cost of every residual
   timeout saga; the 14-17 s floor is pending rig worst-case-turnaround calibration — calibrate
   first, then move both timers together.
8. **Margin-aware laddering:** the Good@22 cells (mean 2303, +22%) put the 16QAM crossover at
   ~20-22 dB effective: ride 16QAM at/above it, QPSK below — the mil/leader ladder shape.
   Preconditions: 3b closed on hardware, AND BUG-DOPPLER-COHERENCE-MODECHANGE-WIPE (KNOWN_BUGS
   names it a precondition for ULTRA_RATE_ADAPT-class mid-stream moves).

**Arithmetic sanity (corrected by the review):** 16QAM R2/3 cw16 ceiling ~3.6-4.0k × ~0.85
damage survival (assumes items 4+8 get damage to ~15% — a projection, gated on item 4's
measurement) × ~0.9 realization ≈ **2.7-3.1 kbps delivered — marginal against 3000, not
comfortably above it**, and conditional on 3b resolving on hardware. Items 4+5+6 are jointly
load-bearing; none suffices alone. If the stack lands short, the remaining honest headroom is
the in-frame overhead diet (§2.3: pilot/LTS/probe rework — the leader's real edge) — a larger
build. No lever above touches the 8-bit SACK mask (wire) or the 8.6 s duty cap (physics/policy).

## 6. Dead ends re-confirmed or newly established (do not re-propose)

- Code-rate climbing at QPSK (R3/4≈R2/3 sim+rig; R5/6 retired), constellation climbing without
  damage work (QAM16 climb = tie), eps_H for QAM16 (double-counts softGrayZone), LLR

  channel-class gating (disproved on rig — reactive only), `ULTRA_BURST_GROUP_FRAMES` as a

  8-frame bursts), naive RTO cuts below the calibrated floor (documented premature-resend
  regression), ACK lead-in trims without rig FER validation (clipped-Costas risk).
- **v3 medium-window predicted-anchor search** — built, unit-tested 20/20, then REVERTED
  2026-06-20 as rig-measured redundant (predict-ON 1.63 ≈ OFF 1.60 kbps; near-live cold/warm
  wins first). A session attacking the §4 acquisition collapse is the precise audience that
  would rebuild it — don't.
- **Gate-less mid-stream rate change** (escape-drop, abort-coordinated requeue) — built, A/B'd,
  REJECTED 2026-06-09; the clean-boundary gate is MANDATORY (mid-stream renumber deadlocks the
  receiver's in-order ARQ). Relevant to anyone acting on §1's "rate controller default-OFF" or
  §5 item 8.
